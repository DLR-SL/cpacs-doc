The decision underneath

Placement cannot be settled on its own. A row that carries an instance path needs about 1,030 px before three paths in four fit whole — the tree column will never have that, which is why 0.9 % fit there today. A row that carries a name and a count of its places needs a fraction of it: 2,223 distinct element names stand behind 53,691 entries, and ToDos.md already asks whether the results should be grouped that way.

So: if the results stay path-carrying, A is cosmetic and D is the only real answer. If they become name-carrying, A is enough and D is overbuilt. The grouping decision comes first; this page is here to make its consequence visible.
